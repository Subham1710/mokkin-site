Why Mokkin

Software can now be built faster than ever. Is it being built for what the business actually needs?

Founders and organisations can reach more agencies, freelancers and app builders than ever. Most software still goes wrong long before the first line of code, in the gap between what you mean and what a team hears.

  • Clarity on what to build first

  • A partner who stays after launch after

How we work

We don't reject speed or code. We change the order they arrive in.

Every project moves through the same four steps. One product owner stays with you from the first conversation to launch, and after it.

  1. Step 01

    Understand

    We ask about your business before your features: who it serves, how it runs today, what is getting in the way.

    a written problem statement

  2. Step 02

    Picture

    We show you mock-ups you can point at and react to, with clear options and prices, before anything is built.

    mock-ups and priced options

  3. Step 03

    Code

    One senior team designs and engineers what you approved. You can talk to the people building it directly.

    a working product, deployed

  4. Step 04

    Care

    Launch care is included, and ongoing care is there for everything we build. We stay after the handover.

    updates, fixes and support
