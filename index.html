How we work

See it before you build it.

Shape, build, care. One product owner from the first conversation to launch, and after it.

  1. 01 · Shape

    We ask first.

    Questions about your business come before talk of features. No question is too basic.

  2. 02 · Shape

    We write it down.

    A problem statement in plain language. You agree it before anything is designed.

  3. 03 · Shape

    You see it.

    A mock-up you can point at and react to. Change your mind while change is cheap.

  4. 04 · Shape

    You choose.

    Clear options with clear prices. We recommend one, tell you why, and leave the decision with you.

  5. 05 · Build

    We build it.

    One senior team designs and engineers exactly what you saw. You can talk to them directly.

  6. 06 · Build

    We launch it.

    Hosting, app store release and a launch plan, explained in plain words. No surprises, because you saw it first.

  7. 07 · Care

    We take care of it.

    Launch care: 60 days of hands-on support included. After that, ongoing care for everything we built.
